Privacy Policy – Islamic Clips44

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Islamic Clips44 ("we") is an application that creates short Islamic videos and uploads them as drafts to the owner's TikTok account.

Information we collect: When you authorize the app with TikTok, we receive your TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and an access token that allows uploading videos as drafts to your account. We do not collect passwords.

How we use information: Your TikTok access token is used only to upload videos you approved as drafts to your TikTok account. We do not share your information with third parties. We do not sell personal data.

Data storage: Access tokens are stored securely and used only for the upload function. You may revoke access at any time from your TikTok settings, which immediately stops the app from acting on your account.

Contact: For questions about this policy, contact us through our TikTok account @rahimbzphw6.
